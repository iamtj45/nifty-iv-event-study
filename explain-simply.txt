===========================================================================
  NIFTY IV EVENT STUDY  --  the whole thing, in simple words
===========================================================================


THE UMBRELLA SHOP
-----------------

Tomorrow the weather office is announcing something big. Everyone's
nervous. They rush out and buy umbrellas.

Umbrella prices shoot up.

Next day comes. It drizzles a bit. Nothing dramatic.

Everyone who panic-bought an umbrella overpaid.


THAT'S YOUR PROJECT
-------------------

  umbrella          an option (insurance against the market moving)
  umbrella price    implied volatility -- how scared people are
  the rain          how much the Nifty actually moved

You measured 29 of those announcement days -- RBI interest-rate
decisions, Union Budgets, and the 2024 election result.

Each time you checked two things:

  1. How much were umbrellas going for the day before?
  2. How much did it actually rain?

RESULT:  19 times out of 23, people had overpaid.

  Before RBI meetings the market priced in 13.35% of movement.
  What actually arrived was 9.87%.


THEN YOU ASKED THREE MORE THINGS
--------------------------------

  "Was that just luck?"

    You took 448 ordinary days with no announcement anywhere near.
    Picked 23 at random. Averaged them. Did that 10,000 times.

    None of the 10,000 looked like this.

    Not luck.


  "So could I run the umbrella shop and get rich?"

    You tested it: sell umbrellas before every announcement, buy them
    back the next day.

    It made almost nothing -- 0.12 points before any fees. Less than
    doing the same thing on a random Tuesday (5.58 points).

    Why? Because on announcement days it DOES rain a bit more than
    usual. Not as much as people feared -- but enough that paying out
    eats what you collected.


  "Does the umbrella price tell me if it'll rain or shine?"

    No. 11 times the market went up, 12 times down. A coin toss.

    The price only ever says "something might happen".
    It never says which way.


THE WHOLE THING IN ONE LINE
---------------------------

  People overpay for protection before big news -- provably, not by
  luck -- but you still can't make money selling it to them.


WHY THAT'S A GOOD RESULT
------------------------

If it had said "yes, easy money", the first question anyone asks is:

  "Thousands of professionals watch this market every day.
   Why hasn't one of them noticed?"

You'd have no answer.

Instead you can say:

  "They have noticed. That's exactly why the price of the rain eats
   the profit."


===========================================================================
  TWO THINGS PEOPLE GET CONFUSED ABOUT
===========================================================================


1. "The answer box said the market rose 0.59% -- I thought we couldn't
    predict direction?"

   Two different things.

     "Which way did it go on 5 Dec 2025?"   +0.59%   -- we measured it
     "Which way will it go next meeting?"    no idea -- coin flip

   Looking back, we know every single day exactly.
   Looking forward, nothing.

   And note the useful asymmetry for a FUTURE event:

     which way will it move?   unknown. 11 up, 12 down.
     how far will it move?     less than the market expects,
                               19 times out of 23.

   Direction is unpredictable. Size is predictable -- not the exact
   number, but the reliable fact that it'll be smaller than people
   are paying for.


2. "Does volatility tell us the direction or not?"

   It tells you TODAY'S direction, strongly. It tells you nothing
   about tomorrow.

     fear went up today   -> market fell today, 62% of the time
     fear went down today -> market rose today, 66% of the time
     biggest fear spikes  -> next day was 54.6% up,
                             against 52.8% on any ordinary day
                             (basically the same)

   Fear rising and prices falling are the same event seen two ways.
   People aren't predicting a drop -- they're reacting to the one on
   their screen.

   A thermometer tells you it's hot right now, very accurately.
   It tells you nothing about tomorrow's weather.


===========================================================================
  THE NUMBERS, IF SOMEONE ASKS
===========================================================================

  Coverage        969 trading days, Dec 2022 - Nov 2026
                  29 events, 2 indices (Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty)

  The finding     RBI:    13.35% priced in, 9.87% delivered,
                          overpriced 19 of 23, IV fell 5.62% on the day
                  Budget: 17.99% priced in, 13.34% delivered

  Not luck        placebo test: 0.04 percentile
                  (of 10,000 random groups, none as extreme)
                  95% range on the RBI fall: -8.88% to -2.70%
                  -- entirely below zero

  Not tradeable   selling the straddle before RBI meetings:
                  0.12 points before costs
                  selling on an ordinary day: 5.58 points
                  worst single loss: -722 points, election day 2024

  No direction    RBI days rose 47.8% of the time
                  ordinary days rose 54.7% of the time
                  so an RBI day tells you LESS than a random Tuesday

  Is it right?    our volatility series correlates 0.9157 with India
                  VIX, which NSE publishes independently using a
                  completely different method

  Second index    Bank Nifty, run through the same pipeline, reaches
                  the same conclusions -- overpriced 21 of 23, and a
                  straddle trade that loses money before costs


===========================================================================
  WHAT THE PROJECT HONESTLY CANNOT DO
===========================================================================

  - See inside a day. It uses end-of-day prices only, so "what
    happened at 2pm" is invisible.

  - Say anything reliable about Budgets. There are only five. With
    five events a statistical test cannot reach significance however
    the data falls.

  - Predict any single future event. The averages hold across 23
    meetings; both indices fell together on only 12 of them.

  - Claim the effect is new. Volatility falling after scheduled
    events is well documented. What's new here is the measurement on
    Indian data -- validated against an outside source, and shown to
    be untradeable.
